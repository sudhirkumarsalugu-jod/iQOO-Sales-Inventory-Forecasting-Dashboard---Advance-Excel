# iQOO-Sales-Inventory-Forecasting-Dashboard---Advance-Excel
This project is about IQOO's Demand and Supply Chain Management.
## Project Overview

An interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to analyze iQOO sales performance, inventory distribution, and demand forecasting. The project focuses on monitoring key operational KPIs, identifying inventory imbalances, and translating business data into actionable insights for inventory planning and management reporting.

The dashboard demonstrates practical skills in **MIS reporting, KPI tracking, data analysis, inventory monitoring, and Excel dashboard development**.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor inventory health through overstock, understock, and stockout metrics.
- Evaluate forecast accuracy across regions and identify demand-planning gaps.
- Compare actual demand with forecasted demand to identify seasonal patterns.
- Identify products contributing disproportionately to stockout events.
- Support inventory allocation and replenishment decisions through data-driven reporting.

These indicators provide a consolidated view of forecast performance, inventory imbalance, product availability, and replenishment lead time.

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Inventory Imbalance in Delhi

Despite an overall inventory surplus, Delhi experiences frequent stockouts. This indicates potential issues with regional inventory allocation and demand forecasting rather than a simple shortage of total inventory.

### 2. Low Forecast Accuracy in the East Region

The East region recorded the lowest forecast accuracy at **32%**, highlighting an opportunity to investigate regional demand patterns and improve forecasting processes.

### 3. Seasonal Demand Variation

Actual demand exceeded forecasted demand during **October–November**, indicating a potential festive-season demand spike. This highlights the importance of incorporating seasonal trends into inventory and replenishment planning.

### 4. Product Concentration in Stockout Events

**iQOO 11 and iQOO 12 together accounted for approximately 82% of stockout events.** These products warrant closer monitoring of demand, stock availability, and replenishment requirements.

## Business Recommendations

- Review regional inventory allocation to address stockouts in Delhi despite overall surplus inventory.
- Investigate forecast assumptions and regional demand patterns in the East region.
- Incorporate festive-season demand trends into forecasting and replenishment planning.
- Prioritize stock availability monitoring for iQOO 11 and iQOO 12.
- Track inventory KPIs regularly to identify emerging exceptions and support operational reporting.
